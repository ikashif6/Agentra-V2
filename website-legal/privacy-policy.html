This Privacy Policy explains how Agentra (“Agentra,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit our website, create an account, start a free trial, buy a subscription, or use our product.

Agentra is a customer support and conversational AI platform built for ecommerce businesses. You can use it to manage conversations, tickets, live chat, AI Agent replies, team workflows, and connected store data across channels such as email, messaging apps, and your website chat widget.

Our marketing website is at https://agentraa.com. Our product is available at https://app.agentraa.com and related workspace URLs on the agentraa.com domain.

By using our website or Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Agentra.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers:

  • Visitors to our website
  • People who create accounts, start trials, or buy subscriptions
  • Team members invited into a workspace
  • People who contact us for support or sales
  • End customers who message a business that uses Agentra (we process that data for the business)

2. Our role and your role

When we act as a data controller

We act as a controller for information about our business relationship with you. Examples include your account details, billing relationship, support emails to us, and website analytics.

When we act as a data processor

When a business uses Agentra to handle customer conversations, tickets, chat sessions, store orders, or connected channel messages, that business is usually the controller of its customer data. We process that data on the business’s behalf to provide the Service.

If you are an end customer of a brand using Agentra, please contact that brand for questions about how they use your information. We will help them respond where the law requires us to.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

Depending on how you use Agentra, you may give us:

  • Name and email address
  • Password or other login details
  • Phone number, job title, bio, and profile photo (optional)
  • Company or brand name, website, industry, company size, timezone, locale, and currency
  • Business address and business hours
  • Workspace branding such as logos and colors
  • Onboarding answers about your goals, channels, and store platform
  • Messages you send to our support or sales team
  • Billing contact details needed for invoices and subscription management

3.2 Account, trial, and purchase information

When you start a free trial or buy a plan, we collect the details needed to create your workspace and activate access right away. After you start a trial or complete a purchase, you get instant access to the Service.

We may store plan name, billing cycle (monthly or yearly), trial start and end dates, subscription status, renewal dates, cancellation status, and invoice history.

Payments are processed by Paddle, which acts as Merchant of Record. Paddle collects and processes payment card details and related billing information. We do not store full payment card numbers on our systems. We may receive limited payment metadata from Paddle, such as subscription status, country, tax treatment, and last payment result.

3.3 Workspace content and support data

If you use Agentra for customer support, we process content you and your customers submit, including:

  • Tickets, subjects, statuses, priorities, tags, and internal notes
  • Message content from email, live chat, AI Agent chats, and connected messaging channels
  • File attachments uploaded to tickets or chats
  • Customer names, emails, phone numbers, and channel identifiers shared in conversations
  • Help center content, contact form submissions, and ticket tracking requests
  • Team assignments, roles, departments, and activity inside the workspace

3.4 Connected channels and store data

If you connect third-party tools, we receive the information needed to run those connections. This can include:

  • Email mailbox settings and encrypted credentials or OAuth tokens
  • Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, and similar messaging account details and message content
  • Live chat widget settings, allowed website domains, and chat session content
  • Shopify, WooCommerce, or custom store connection details
  • Order, product, customer, shipping, fulfillment, refund, and policy data synced from your store where enabled

Sensitive credentials are encrypted where the Service supports it.

3.5 Technical and usage information

We automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, and approximate usage patterns on our website and product
  • Login events, session data, and security logs
  • Error reports, API request logs, and abuse-prevention signals

3.6 Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar tools to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure site performance, and protect the Service. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

8. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, including to provide the Service, meet legal and accounting needs, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Unless a longer period is required by law, we apply these periods:

  • Account and workspace data: kept while the workspace is active, then deleted or anonymized within 90 days after closure or a verified deletion request
  • Tickets, live chat sessions, messages, and attachments: kept while the workspace is active; deleted with the workspace within 90 days of closure, or sooner if an authorized user deletes them
  • Synced store order snapshots used for support: kept while the store remains connected and for up to 90 days after disconnect or workspace closure
  • Integration tokens: kept only while the connection remains active, then removed promptly on disconnect
  • Billing and tax records: kept for up to 7 years (or longer if required by law)
  • Security and activity logs: kept for up to 12 months for security monitoring and investigations

Backup copies may remain for up to 30 days after deletion before they are overwritten. Workspace owners may request earlier deletion by emailing agentraa0@gmail.com.

These include encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), encryption at rest for databases and sensitive credentials where supported, password hashing, role-based access controls, session controls, and monitoring for abuse.

No online service is perfectly secure. Please keep your login details private and manage team access carefully inside your workspace. Our incident response procedures are described in our Security Incident Response Policy.

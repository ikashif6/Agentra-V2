This Cookie Policy explains how Agentra (“Agentra,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and product.

Our website is https://agentraa.com. Our product is available at https://app.agentraa.com and related workspace URLs on the agentraa.com domain.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, pixels, and software development kits that store or read information on your device.

Some cookies are needed for basic site and login functions. Others help us understand how the site is used or remember your preferences.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in and protect your session
  • Remember settings and preferences
  • Load pages correctly and keep forms working
  • Prevent fraud, spam, and abuse
  • Understand which pages are useful and where visitors drop off
  • Measure marketing performance where those tools are enabled
  • Support checkout and subscription flows handled by our payment partner, Paddle

3. Types of cookies we use

3.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the website and product to work. They help with security, login, network management, and access to features you request. Without them, account sign-in, trial activation, and purchase confirmation pages may not work properly.

3.2 Functional cookies

These cookies remember choices you make, such as language preference, dismissed notices, or interface settings, so the experience feels consistent when you return.

3.3 Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how people use our website and product. For example, which pages are visited, how long sessions last, and what can be improved. We use this information in aggregated or similar forms to improve Agentra.

3.4 Marketing cookies

Where enabled, these cookies help us measure campaigns, understand whether ads or referral links are working, and show more relevant messages on our own channels. We only use non-essential marketing cookies where allowed by applicable law, including consent where required.

5. First-party and third-party cookies

5.1 First-party cookies

These are set by Agentra on our domains, such as agentraa.com and app.agentraa.com.

5.2 Third-party cookies

These may be set by partners that help us run parts of the Service, for example:

  • Paddle for checkout, subscriptions, invoices, and tax handling
  • Analytics providers that measure website or product usage
  • Email or support tools that need to recognize returning visitors in limited ways
  • Embedded content or connected platform widgets where you choose to use them

Third parties process information under their own privacy and cookie policies as well as any agreement they have with us.

6. Live chat widget cookies

If a business installs the Agentra live chat widget on its storefront or website, the widget may use cookies or local storage on that business’s site to:

  • keep a visitor’s chat session open
  • remember that a conversation already started
  • route messages to the correct workspace

In that case, the business using Agentra is responsible for providing any required cookie notice on its own website. Our Privacy Policy explains how chat data is processed for those workspaces.

7. Your choices

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: most browsers let you block, delete, or restrict cookies
  • Cookie banner or preference tools: where we show them, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies
  • Industry opt-outs: for some advertising cookies, you may use tools offered by ad industry groups in your region

If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the site or product may not work. You may not be able to stay logged in, start a trial smoothly, or complete checkout.

Blocking analytics or marketing cookies will not stop you from receiving instant access after a valid trial start or purchase. Access is delivered through your account, not through marketing cookies.

8. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” signal. There is no single industry standard for responding to it. We treat privacy choices through the controls described in this policy and our Privacy Policy.

9. Data retention linked to cookies

Cookie lifetimes vary by purpose. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain until they expire or you delete them. Information collected through cookies may also be stored in our systems for the periods described in our Privacy Policy.

10. International visitors

If you visit from the EEA, UK, or another region with cookie consent rules, we will ask for consent before using non-essential cookies where required. Strictly necessary cookies may still run because they are needed to provide the Service you request.

11. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our tools, providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we revise it. Please review this page from time to time.

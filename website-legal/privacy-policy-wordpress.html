Agentra is an ecommerce customer-support platform that combines an AI Agent with a helpdesk. Businesses can use Agentra to manage customer conversations, tickets, team workflows, connected channels, customer information and ecommerce store data. Our marketing website is available at agentraa.com. The Agentra platform is available at app.agentraa.com and related workspace addresses on the agentraa.com domain. 7. How Agentra Uses Artificial Intelligence

Agentra uses artificial intelligence to provide customer-support and helpdesk features. Depending on the workspace configuration, AI may be used to:
  • Understand customer questions;
  • Classify requests;
  • Retrieve relevant business information;
  • Generate proposed customer replies;
  • Summarise conversations;
  • Suggest products;
  • Suggest or perform authorised support actions;
  • Recommend conversation routing;
  • Identify urgency, sentiment or potential dissatisfaction; and
  • Assist human support agents.
To provide these features, relevant message content, conversation history, business instructions, product information, order information and customer context may be processed by Agentra and by OpenAI, our AI processing provider. We aim to limit information sent to AI providers to what is reasonably needed for the requested feature. OpenAI processes that information under its service terms and our applicable arrangements. Agentra does not guarantee that AI-generated content will always be accurate, complete or appropriate. Workspace owners are responsible for configuring instructions, permissions, approval requirements and human-handoff rules. Authorised workspace users can review, correct, override or take control of AI-generated responses and actions.

8. Automated Decisions and Human Oversight

Agentra’s AI features are designed to assist with customer support and operational workflows. They are not intended to make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about an individual. AI-generated sentiment, behaviour and churn indicators are predictions or assistance tools. They should be reviewed by authorised users before being used to make important decisions about a customer. Workspace owners can control:
  • Which AI features are enabled;
  • Which information the AI can access;
  • Which actions the AI may take;
  • Which actions require approval;
  • When a human agent must become involved; and
  • Whether an AI-generated reply is sent automatically or reviewed first.

13. Data Deletion and Account Closure

You can request deletion of personal information Agentra controls, or closure of an Agentra workspace, by emailing hello@agentraa.com from the email address connected to the account. Workspace owners and administrators may also:
  • Disconnect Meta, Shopify or other integrations from Agentra settings, which stops new syncing through that connection;
  • Remove team members and revoke access; and
  • Request workspace closure.
When a deletion or closure request is verified, we will delete or anonymise the relevant account and workspace information within 30 days, except where we must retain information for legal, security, dispute-resolution or accounting reasons. Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups until those backups are rotated in the normal course of operations. If you uninstall or disconnect Agentra from Shopify or Meta, new data will stop flowing from that connection. Existing tickets and conversation history already stored in Agentra remain available to the merchant until deleted or the workspace is closed, subject to this Policy. If your request relates to information collected by a business using Agentra (for example, a WhatsApp conversation with a brand), contact that business first. Agentra will assist the business with deletion where required. Meta users may also submit platform data-deletion requests through Meta’s tools where available. Agentra will process valid Meta data-deletion callbacks for information Agentra controls in connection with the Meta app.

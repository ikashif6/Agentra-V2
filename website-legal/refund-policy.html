Refund Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026
Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds, cancellations, and billing issues work for Agentra subscriptions. Please read it together with our Terms and Conditions.

Agentra is a digital software product. When you start a free trial or complete a purchase, you get instant access to your account and workspace online. There is no physical product to return.

1. Instant access to the Service

Agentra is delivered digitally. After you:

  • start a free trial, or
  • complete a paid purchase,

you receive instant access to the Service through your Agentra account. Access usually begins as soon as signup or payment is confirmed.

If access does not appear right away, email agentraa0@gmail.com and we will help restore access as quickly as possible.

2. Free trials

If you are on a free trial, you can cancel at any time during the trial at no charge. Because no payment was collected for the trial period itself, there is nothing to refund for unused trial days.

If your trial converts to a paid subscription and you are charged, the paid-subscription rules below apply from that point.

3. Our refund promise

We want you to feel comfortable trying Agentra. For first-time paid subscriptions, we offer a 14-day refund window from the date of the initial paid charge, subject to the conditions in this policy.

What qualifies

You may request a refund if all of the following are true:

  • The request is for your first paid subscription charge with Agentra
  • You contact us within 14 days of that charge
  • You are not requesting a refund only for unused time on a renewed billing cycle after the initial purchase window
  • Your account has not been suspended for abuse, fraud, or a serious Terms violation

What usually does not qualify

  • Refund requests made more than 14 days after the first paid charge
  • Renewal charges after the initial purchase period, unless required by law or approved by us as an exception
  • Partial refunds for unused days in the middle of a billing period after the refund window has closed
  • Charges caused by forgetting to cancel before renewal, after you had a reasonable chance to cancel
  • Accounts terminated for spam, fraud, abuse, or other serious policy violations
  • Third-party fees, bank fees, currency conversion fees, or taxes that your bank or local rules do not allow us to reverse

4. Yearly plans

Yearly plans also receive instant access after purchase. If you buy a yearly plan and request a refund within the 14-day first-purchase window above, we may refund the yearly charge according to this policy.

After that window, yearly plan fees are generally non-refundable for the remainder of the year, except where local law requires otherwise or we agree to an exception in writing.

5. How to cancel

You can cancel future renewals at any time by:

  • using the billing or subscription controls in your Agentra account, or
  • emailing agentraa0@gmail.com from the email on your account and asking to cancel

Cancellation stops future charges. Unless you also qualify for a refund under this policy, you usually keep access until the end of the period already paid for, and no prorated refund is issued for the remaining days.

6. How to request a refund

To request a refund, email agentraa0@gmail.com with:

  • the email address on your Agentra account
  • your workspace or company name
  • the date of the charge
  • the invoice or transaction reference if you have it
  • a short reason for the request

We review requests in good faith and usually respond within a few business days.

7. How refunds are processed

Payments for Agentra are processed by Paddle, which acts as Merchant of Record. Approved refunds are issued through Paddle back to the original payment method when possible.

Refund timing depends on Paddle and your bank or card issuer. After we approve a refund, it may take several business days to appear on your statement.

Once a refund is completed, we may end or limit access to the paid Service linked to that purchase.

8. Chargebacks

If you have a billing problem, please contact us first so we can help quickly. Filing a chargeback without contacting us can delay resolution and may lead to account suspension while the dispute is reviewed.

If a chargeback is filed, we may provide Paddle and the payment provider with information needed to respond to the dispute.

9. Service issues and goodwill exceptions

If Agentra was unavailable in a major way because of an issue on our side, or if you were charged in error, contact us. We may offer a refund, credit, or other fair remedy even when a request falls outside the standard window.

We may also make goodwill exceptions in rare cases. Those decisions are made case by case and do not change the rest of this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The updated version applies to purchases made after the “Last updated” date. For earlier purchases, the policy in place at the time of purchase still guides how we handle that order, unless the law requires a different approach.

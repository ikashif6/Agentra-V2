Security Incident Response Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026
Last updated: July 26, 2026

This policy describes how Agentra detects, assesses, contains, and remediates security incidents that may affect personal information processed through the Agentra Service.

1. What counts as a security incident

A security incident includes unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, or loss of personal information; ransomware or other malicious compromise of Service systems; or a confirmed breach of credentials that can access customer or merchant data.

2. Detection and reporting

Incidents may be identified through monitoring, abuse alerts, partner or merchant reports, and internal reviews. Anyone who suspects an incident should email agentraa0@gmail.com immediately.

3. Response process

3.1 Triage and containment

We assess severity and impact, then take reasonable steps to contain the incident (for example revoking credentials, isolating systems, or rotating secrets).

3.2 Investigation

We investigate root cause, determine what personal information may have been affected, and preserve relevant logs.

3.3 Remediation

We remediate the issue, restore affected services where needed, and verify that unauthorized access has stopped.

3.4 Notification

Where personal information is reasonably believed to have been compromised, we notify affected merchants without undue delay and, where required by law, no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of a personal data breach. Notifications will describe, to the extent known, the nature of the incident, categories of data involved, likely consequences, measures taken, and how to contact us.

Merchants remain responsible for notifying their own end customers where they are the controller, unless law requires us to notify individuals directly.

4. Access during incidents

Access to systems and personal data during an incident is limited to personnel who need it to investigate and remediate. Staff access requires strong authentication.

5. Records and improvement

We document material incidents, response actions, and lessons learned, and use those findings to improve monitoring and controls.

6. Contact

Security or privacy incident reports: agentraa0@gmail.com
